01

TECHNICAL SEO · CONTENT SEO

Client

SEO work across a large business setup website targeting competitive commercial searches in Dubai.

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge was to strengthen visibility beyond branded searches and expand organic reach across non-branded, high-intent queries spanning commercial pages, business activities, relocation content, and supporting resources.

THE WORK

Identifying high-value non-branded queries that could drive organic traffic, analysing their search intent and performance, and aligning existing pages with those opportunities to expand visibility beyond branded searches.
