A selection of SEO projects covering technical SEO, content strategy, keyword research, local search, and search performance.

01

TECHNICAL SEO · CONTENT SEO

Meydan Free Zone

SEO work across a large business setup website targeting competitive commercial searches in Dubai.

THE CHALLENGE

Competing for high-intent searches across commercial pages, business activity pages, blogs, relocation content, and supporting resources.

THE WORK

Technical SEO, keyword research, content strategy, metadata, internal linking, structured data, activity pages, and local search optimisation.

SEARCH FOCUS

Business setup, company formation, free zone licensing, business activities, relocation, and local search.

FOCUS

Improving organic visibility across both high-volume commercial searches and more specific activity-related queries.

02

KEYWORD RESEARCH · SEO STRATEGY

M.A.H.Y Khoory Group

Developing an SEO strategy for a diversified UAE business group with multiple companies and business verticals.

THE CHALLENGE

Establish search visibility for the parent group while maintaining clear separation between the group and its individual businesses.

THE WORK

Keyword research around corporate searches, partnerships, recruitment, regional demand, brand visibility, and searches related to distribution partners.

STRATEGIC FOCUS

Brand awareness, corporate search, partnership searches, recruitment, regional visibility, and information architecture.

OBJECTIVE

Build a stronger organic presence for the parent group and identify search opportunities around its wider business relationships.

03

LOCAL SEO

Google Business Profile

Supporting local search visibility through Google Business Profile optimisation and website signals.

THE CHALLENGE

Local visibility depends on consistency between the business profile, website, services, location, and search demand.

THE WORK

Google Business Profile posts, images, videos, service information, local content, and supporting internal links.

SEARCH FOCUS

Dubai business setup, free zone services, local business searches, and service-related queries.

APPROACH

Local SEO is treated as part of the wider search strategy rather than as a standalone profile optimisation task.

04

CONTENT STRATEGY · KEYWORD RESEARCH

Search-Led Content Strategy

Turning search demand into a structured content pipeline based on intent, rankings, gaps, and commercial relevance.

THE CHALLENGE

Publishing more content does not automatically create more organic visibility. Topics need to match actual search demand.

THE WORK

Keyword research, search intent analysis, query fan-out, search volume, ranking data, content gaps, and internal linking.

OUTPUT

Content topics, keyword targets, optimisation opportunities, internal linking recommendations, and priorities.

PRINCIPLE

Content volume is not the objective. The goal is to create pages that satisfy search intent and contribute to the wider SEO strategy.
