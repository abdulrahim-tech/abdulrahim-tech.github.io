ABOUT ME

Who I am & What I do

I'm Rahim, an SEO Specialist based in Dubai.

I'm naturally curious and tend to learn about whatever catches my attention. Psychology is one of those interests. I enjoy reading about why people think, behave, and make decisions the way they do.

I don't really have one specific subject that I like learning about. If something interests me, I'll probably end up going down a rabbit hole trying to understand it.

I'm also a HUGE fan of cats, partly because you never quite know what they're going to do next.

Professionally, I work across content SEO, keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, internal linking, and SEO analytics. I've worked on projects across industries including business setup, aviation, hospitality, and tourism.

What I enjoy most about SEO is the problem-solving side of it. There's usually something to investigate, something to question, or something that could be done better. I'm still learning and experimenting, which is probably what keeps the work interesting.
